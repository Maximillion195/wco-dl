Library gaps

Linked shows with episodes missing or in another language only (a real count), and other animated series Sonarr says are missing episodes, matched to wcostream by name. Live action is left out: wcostream does not carry it.

Following a show links it and lets the overnight run (between 01:00 and 07:00, 120 episodes a night, shortest lists first) fill its gaps. Nothing downloads when you press Follow.
